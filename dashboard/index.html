How the value is modelled

One formula scores every row. Nothing in it names a protocol.

implied = TVL × peerMultiple(category)
        × (1 + clamp(change_7d, −50%, +150%) × 0.35)
        × (0.75 + 0.25 × min(ageDays, 1095) / 1095)
        × (1 + 0.02 × min(chains − 1, 5))

What “records no token” means

A row says a protocol records no token: DefiLlama's snapshot carries no CoinGecko id, no symbol and no market cap for it. That is not proof it has none — a token may exist that the record does not carry. Rows the house watchlist leaves out are flagged Early find: they pass the same no-token rule, but DefiLlama's parent-protocol record (not one of the snapshot's fields) may link one to another protocol, so verify it before farming.

What the radar does not do

It does not predict whether or when a token is issued. An implied value is this entry's own claim about what a launch would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is. Farm with a fresh wallet, never pay to claim, and never put in more than you can afford to leave locked for the hold period.
